Data & privacy

Your check-ins are saved in this browser on this device using local storage. They are not sent to a server. Clearing browser data or using a different browser/device may remove or hide your records, so export a backup periodically.

Scoring rules

Each Yes earns one point. Pillar scores are the percentage of Yes answers within that pillar. The daily total is the average of the four pillar percentages, so every pillar has equal weight—even if one has more questions.

Ranks: A · 85–100 B · 70–84 C · 55–69 D · 40–54 E · below 40

Missing answers are treated as unanswered, not automatically as No. A day is scored only after you save it. Compassion includes both kindness to others and to yourself.

Reset local data

This permanently deletes saved check-ins and reflections from this browser. Export a backup first if you may want your history later.
